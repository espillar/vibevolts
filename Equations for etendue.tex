\section{Symbols}
\begin{itemize}

    \item $\alpha$ is the brightness of the target
    being sought in units commensurate with
    $\mathrm{photons/second/meter^2}$
    \item $\beta$ is the brightness of the sky
    ($\mathrm{photons/second/meter^2/steradian}$)
    \item $\gamma$ will be the SNR ratio
    \item $\Omega$ is the area of the  FOV of the sensor/optical system ($\mathrm{steradians^2}$)
    \item $\omega$ is the area of the  FOV of a pixel ($\mathrm{steradians^2}$) or the seeing disk or the diffraction disk,
    whichever is largest. Basically this is the area over which
    the light is spread and needs to compete with the
    background. 
    \item $A$ is the effective collecting area 
    ($\mathrm{meter^2}$).
    \item $T$ is the integration time (seconds.) This may be the result
    	of processing $n$ shorter integrations $t.$ 
     \item $g$ is used in some places as the goal limiting magitude
	\item $q$ is the net quantum efficiency of the whole system
	\item $f$ is the fraction of light from an object captured
			in the photometry aperture
	\item $C$ gives the number of photons per second per squar meter for the band being used.
\end{itemize}

\section{Derivation of Search Rate}

The signal from an object is 
$S \propto \alpha A T.$
Assuming we are background limited, the noise is
$N \propto \sqrt{\beta A T \omega}. $
Putting things together
$$SNR^2 = \gamma^2 \propto 
{\alpha^2 A^2 T^2 \over \beta A T \omega} 
= {\alpha^2 A T \over \beta   \omega}  \ \ \mathrm{so } \ \
{1\over T} \propto {\alpha^2 A \over \gamma^2 \beta \omega }.$$
This is the rate at which fields are interrogated for objects to the desired `limiting magnitude."  To get the search rate,  multiply by the total area surveyed:

\begin{equation} 
\boxed{SR = {\Omega \over T} \propto 
{\alpha^2 A  \Omega \over \gamma^2 \beta \omega }}
\end{equation}
which has units commensurate with steradians/second.


\section{Integration Time Required for an SNR}
Given a few things I want to find the integration
time needed for a given SNR, assuming for the moment BLIP.

Factors we have in the code:
$g$ is our goal limiting magnitude.
$$S = q f A T \amag{g} C $$
$$N = \sqrt{q A t \amag{\beta} C \omega} $$

$$SNR^2 = \frac{q^2 f^2 A^2 t^2 \amag{g}^2 C^2 }{q A t \amag{\beta} C \omega} $$
$$= \frac{q f^2 A t\amag{g}^2 C }
		  {   \amag{\beta}  \omega} $$
solving for $t$

\begin{equation}
\boxed{ 
T = \frac{   \amag{\beta}  \omega}{{q f^2 A
\amag{g}^2 C }}
}
\end{equation}
